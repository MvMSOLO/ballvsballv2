# BALL ARENA — Brand usage guide

## Identity
Ball Arena is an original physics-duel game brand. The angular arena sigil represents a built arena, an impact point, and a forward path. It is a custom identity and must not be presented as an official REDMAGIC or Roblox asset.

## Core assets
- `ball-arena-wordmark.svg`: primary horizontal lockup for desktop headers, landing pages, creator media, and wider placements.
- `ball-arena-mark.svg`: compact sigil for navigation, tiny placements, and contexts where the wordmark is not legible.
- `favicon.svg`: browser identity; preserve strong silhouette and contrast at tiny sizes.
- `ball-arena-loading.svg`: loading and wait states. Avoid placing multiple animated marks on one screen; provide a still state when reduced motion is requested.
- `season-01-origin.svg`: Season 01 launch badge / promotional stamp. Do not imply ranked-season rewards are active unless the related feature is live.

## Visual language
- Void graphite `#080A0E` and machined panels establish depth; reserve clear space around the sigil.
- Signal red `#FF4D37` denotes primary action, impact, and identity; electric blue `#4AA3FF` communicates information/focus; gold `#FFC96B` signals rewards/value; green `#61E6AC` means success only.
- Use Chakra Petch for strong display lines, Inter for readable interface copy, and IBM Plex Mono for compact telemetry.
- Use high contrast, deliberate edges, and restrained glow. Glow is an accent, not a default border treatment.
- Do not squash, tilt, recolor, outline, or add effects to official logo files. Do not replace wordmark text with a generic gaming font in campaign artwork.

## Placement rules
- Leave at least the width of the inner sigil gap around a stand-alone mark.
- Use the horizontal wordmark at approximately 150–360 px width; below that, use the standalone mark instead.
- On textured/3D backgrounds, ensure a stable dark backing or clear contrast zone. Do not place thin lines directly over busy particles.
- Do not use the season badge as the main company/product logo.
- Ad thumbnails should include one dominant gameplay moment, one short headline authored separately, and the wordmark; avoid stacking multiple decorative badges.
- Never fabricate online player counts, ratings, testimonials, purchases, or PvP availability in marketing materials.

## Export and accessibility
SVG assets are vector and should remain SVG in product UI. Provide descriptive alternative text when the logo conveys information; use empty alt text when adjacent visible text already identifies the product. Decorative loading animation must not be the only status communication.
